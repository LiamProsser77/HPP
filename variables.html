LanScript Commands

<lanscript> Starts a LanScript line.

<pro> Everything in the script is part of the program.

<start> Starts the program.

<end> Ends the program.

yes.put. Puts something into the program like text.

<script> Ends the script and moves to the next line.

name. Adds a name.

age. Sets the age of a person or website.

.up Makes words uppercase.

.down Makes words lowercase.

.half Cuts words in half.
